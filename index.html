A Pioneering Vision

A pioneering conservation tourism project, spectacularly set in one of India's most enchanting wildernesses.

Spend unforgettable days exploring rural Rajasthan as you immerse yourself in the local culture whilst tracking leopards and other wildlife. Blending sophistication and ecology, there is no better place to discover one of India's most enchanting and breathtakingly beautiful landscapes.

Wilderness Experiences

Wilderness Safaris

Up close encounters with Indian wildlife.

On twice-daily wilderness drives through the Jawai wilderness, our expert guides will educate you on the region's rich plant and animal life, ecology, and rich cultural history, sharing deep connections with the natural beauty of the ecosystem. We have one of the world's highest densities of leopard roaming the hills along with antelope, boar, and over 245 species of birds.

Culture & Nature Entwined

A Unique Safari Experience

Framed by granite hills and the ancient Aravalli range, the SUJÁN Jawai experience is defined by the magical intermingling of wildlife and rural village life, coexisting peacefully for centuries. Encounters with wildlife are seamlessly complemented by meaningful engagements with local communities and deep immersion in the local culture.

Village walks, horse riding, hiking, temple visits and walking safaris with the Rabari tribe offer just some of the ways to connect with this magical land.

SUJÁN Jawai is located near Jawai Bandh in the Pali district of Rajasthan. The nearest airport is Jodhpur (approximately 100 km away) and Udaipur (approximately 160 km). Our team will coordinate private transfers on request. Detailed GPS coordinates and route maps are included in our guest confirmation package.

  • Nearest airport: Jodhpur (JDH) — 100 km
  • Nearest town: Bali — 15 km
  • Private transfers available from Jodhpur & Udaipur

Health & Travel Documents

Vaccinations: Guests are advised to check the latest government requirements and information regarding vaccinations before travel. None of SUJÁN's properties are situated in high malaria-risk areas. However, it is essential that guests consult their medical practitioners regarding anti-malaria requirements prior to travel.

Visas: All foreign citizens will require a visa to travel to India. Please consult your travel agent or contact our reservations team for more information.

For your safari we advise sticking to a tonal, neutral colour palette (khaki, beige, light brown) and avoiding dark colours as they absorb heat and bright colours as they can attract insects.

  • Long trousers & khakis for safari; long-sleeve cotton shirts
  • Trousers or shorts for around camp
  • Walking shoes / hiking boots for walks
  • Wide-brimmed hat / panama
  • Windbreaker / light jacket; light fleece for evenings
  • Sunglasses, lip balm, mosquito repellent
  • Binoculars (we also provide them)

Winter months (Nov–Feb): Pack a warm jacket or winter coat, thick jumpers, beanie, scarf, gloves, long-sleeve shirts and thermals.

Jawai enjoys a semi-arid climate influenced by its proximity to the Thar Desert. Summers (Apr–Jun) are hot and dry; the best wildlife viewing season is October to March when temperatures are pleasant.

  • Oct – Feb: 10°C – 28°C, cool evenings, ideal for safaris
  • Mar – Apr: 20°C – 36°C, warm and dry
  • May – Jun: 30°C – 42°C, very hot
  • Jul – Sep: Monsoon season, lush landscape
